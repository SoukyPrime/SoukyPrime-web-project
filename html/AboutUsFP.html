Our Story

GreenTech Solutions was founded in 2018 in San Francisco by a team of engineers and environmentalists who shared a simple but urgent belief: technology had a responsibility to help reverse the damage done to our planet. What began as a university research project on industrial energy waste quickly grew into a full-fledged startup with a clear mission — to make sustainability accessible, measurable, and effortless for businesses everywhere. Today, we serve over 500 companies across North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia, helping them reduce emissions, improve resource efficiency, and meet their ESG reporting obligations. We're a team of over 80 engineers, ecologists, designers, and sustainability experts who come to work every day driven by the same goal: a cleaner, healthier world powered by smarter technology.
